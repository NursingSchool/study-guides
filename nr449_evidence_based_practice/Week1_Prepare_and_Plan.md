## Weekly Outcomes

1. Assess the impact of evidence-based practice on standards of care. (CO 4)
2. Review research findings to determine the relevance of the evidence to one's own practice. (CO 2)
3. Discuss nursing research and its evolution. (CO 5)
4. Evaluate ethical and legal guidelines when obtaining sources of research-based evidence. (CO 1)
5. Identify historical events influencing the development of codes of ethics and regulation of research. (CO 3)
6. Evaluate the potential for scientific misconduct in reporting and publishing healthcare research. (CO 3)
7. Examine ethical principles guiding research on human subjects. (CO 3)
8. Value the role of research findings in evidence-based practice. (CO 5)

---

## Weekly Objectives

1. Define nursing research.
2. Understand the evolution of nursing research.
3. Differentiate nursing research versus evidence-based practice.
4. Investigate the roles that nurses play in research processes.
5. Explore how research is used as evidence to support the practice of nursing.
6. Discuss ways that research literature can be used as evidence for nursing education and practice.
7. Discuss ethical concepts applicable to human subjects research.
8. Explain the historical events that influenced the development of codes of ethics and regulation of research.
9. Describe the components of informed consent.
10. Identify characteristics of a vulnerable population in a research context.
11. Discuss types of scientific misconduct in a research setting.
12. Discuss the privacy rule (HIPAA) that affects data collection for research.
13. Apply ethical principles in a research context.
14. Reflect on ways that research findings can be used as evidence for nursing practice.
