## Weekly Outcomes

1. Identify essential growth and development milestones. (COs 1 and 4)
2. Apply age-appropriate nursing assessment techniques in providing care for children and families. (COs 1, 3, 4, 6, and 7)
3. Incorporate individual and family learning needs to optimize care. (COs 1, 2, 3, 6, and 7)
4. Utilize available resources for personal, professional, and educational development in pediatric settings. (CO 5)
5. Identify alterations in health for children. (COs 1, 2, 7, and 8)
6. Apply evidence-based practice to care for children. (COs 4, 5, and 8)

## Weekly Objectives

1. Recognize the impact of personal beliefs and values on providing culturally-appropriate, family-centered nursing care.
2. Compare health practices of different cultural or ethnic groups.
3. Discuss characteristics of family-centered care.
4. Identify social, cultural, spiritual, and family influences on children's health promotion.
5. Recognize the professional responsibility and the decision-making role when providing and planning developmentally appropriate, pediatric nursing care.
6. Describe physiological and behavioral responses to pain at different developmental ages.
7. Discuss pain management in children.
8. Employ developmentally appropriate communication, teaching, and assessment techniques.
9. Apply age-specific strategies in nursing care of children.
10. Recognize leading causes of injury and strategies for injury prevention for children across ages.

### Online Resources

- [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Child Development Facts](https://www.cdc.gov/child-development/about/?CDC_AAref_Val=https://www.cdc.gov/ncbddd/childdevelopment/facts.html): This website is a comprehensive collection of facts about child development, including developmental screening, positive parenting tips, mental health, data and statistics, promotional materials, and links to other informational websites.
- [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Child Development Screening](https://www.cdc.gov/child-development/?CDC_AAref_Val=https://www.cdc.gov/ncbddd/childdevelopment/screening-hcp.html): This website outlines the importance of early intervention services, as well as providing educational material on developmental delay identification and treatment.
- [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention Immunization Schedule](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/?CDC_AAref_Val=https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/schedules/index.html): This website is an excellent resource for the most up-to-date immunization recommendations and schedules. Both adult and pediatric schedules can be downloaded in an easy-to-read format.
- [Society for Pediatric Pain Medicine](https://pedspainmedicine.org/): The Society for Pediatric Pain Medicine is organized within the Society for Pediatric Anesthesia. It is a collaboration of key leaders of acute and chronic pediatric pain programs. The society provides a newsletter, publications, and links of interest.
- [Child Pain](http://ppl.childpain.org/): This website is from a special interest group (SIG) for pain in childhood that offers a free monthly newsletter.
