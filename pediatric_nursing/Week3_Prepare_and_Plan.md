## Weekly Outcomes

1. Apply the nursing process when caring for children with circulatory system alterations. (COs 1, 3, 4, 6, and 7)
2. Identify alterations in health for children with circulatory system alterations. (COs 1, 2, 7, and 8)
3. Identify growth and development and health promotion needs for children with circulatory system alterations. (COs 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7)
4. Develop family-centered nursing care for children with circulatory system alterations. (COs 2, 3, 6, and 7)
5. Apply evidence-based practice to care for children with circulatory system alterations. (COs 4, 5, and 8)

## Weekly Objectives

1. Describe hemodynamics, risk factors, clinical manifestations, and diagnostic evaluation of children with cardiovascular and hematologic alterations.
2. Discuss pharmacologic and non-pharmacologic strategies when caring for children with cardiovascular and hematologic alterations.
3. Examine the nursing role in diagnostic procedures when caring for children with cardiovascular and hematologic alterations.
4. Determine nursing care for children with cardiovascular and hematologic alterations.
5. Conduct assessment of cardiac function when caring for children with cardiovascular and hematologic alterations.
