## Weekly Outcomes

1. Identify alterations in health for children with musculoskeletal and neuromuscular system alterations. (COs 1, 2, 7, and 8)
2. Apply the nursing process when caring for children with musculoskeletal and neuromuscular system alterations. (COs 1, 3, 4, 6, and 7)
3. Identify growth and development and health promotion needs for children with musculoskeletal and neuromuscular system alterations. (COs 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7)
4. Develop family-centered nursing care for children with musculoskeletal and neuromuscular system alterations. (COs 2, 3, 6, and 7)
5. Apply evidence-based practice to care for children with musculoskeletal and neuromuscular system alterations. (COs 4, 5, and 8)

## Weekly Objectives

1. Describe risk factors, clinical manifestations, and diagnostic evaluation of children with musculoskeletal and neuromuscular alterations.
2. Discuss pharmacologic and non-pharmacologic strategies when caring for children with musculoskeletal and neuromuscular alterations.
3. Determine nursing care for children with musculoskeletal and neuromuscular alterations.
4. Evaluate the musculoskeletal and neuromuscular status in infants and children, including history and physical assessment.
5. Recognize leading causes of injury and strategies for injury prevention for children across ages.
