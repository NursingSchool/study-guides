## Weekly Outcomes

1. Apply the nursing process when caring for children with respiratory system alterations or communicable diseases. (COs 1, 3, 4, 6, and 7)
2. Identify alterations in health for children with respiratory system alterations or communicable diseases. (COs 1, 2, 7, and 8)
3. Identify growth and development and health promotion needs for children with respiratory system alterations or communicable diseases. (COs 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7)
4. Develop family-centered nursing care for children with respiratory system alterations or communicable diseases. (COs 2, 3, 6, and 7)
5. Apply evidence-based practice to care for children with respiratory system alterations or communicable diseases. (COs 4, 5, and 8)

## Weekly Objectives

1. Apply developmentally appropriate nursing interventions when providing family-centered care for a pediatric client with altered respiratory function or communicable disease.
2. Integrate developmentally appropriate teaching strategies when caring for a pediatric client with altered respiratory function or communicable disease.
3. Examine health promotion as it relates to communicable diseases of childhood and pediatric clients with respiratory alterations.
4. Discuss routine childhood immunizations.
