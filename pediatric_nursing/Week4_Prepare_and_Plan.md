## Weekly Outcomes

1. Apply the nursing process when caring for children with alterations in gastrointestinal function and elimination. (COs 1, 3, 4, 6, and 7)
2. Identify growth and development and health promotion needs for children with alterations in gastrointestinal function and elimination. (COs 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7)
3. Identify alterations in health for children with alterations in gastrointestinal function and elimination. (COs 1, 2, 7, and 8)
4. Develop family-centered nursing care for children with alterations in gastrointestinal function and elimination. (COs 2, 3, 6, and 7)
5. Apply evidence-based practice to care for children with alterations gastrointestinal function and elimination. (COs 4, 5, and 8)

## Weekly Objectives

1. Describe risk factors, clinical manifestations, and diagnostic evaluation of children with alterations in gastrointestinal function and elimination.
2. Discuss pharmacologic and non-pharmacologic strategies when caring for children with alterations in gastrointestinal function and elimination.
3. Determine nursing care for children with alterations in gastrointestinal function and elimination.
